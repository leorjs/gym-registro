# Gym Registro Design System

## Product Direction

Gym Registro is a premium training log for people who use it between sets, mostly on a phone. The interface should feel athletic, precise, and fast, not like a generic SaaS dashboard.

## Visual Rules

- Use a warm graphite surface, crisp off-white panels, and controlled electric green accents.
- Prefer dense, scannable training data over decorative hero sections.
- Use strong numeric typography for volume, streaks, reps, weight, and records.
- Keep cards to actual repeated entities or tool surfaces. Do not nest cards.
- Use icons for navigation and compact actions.
- Avoid purple gradients, glassmorphism, giant marketing hero blocks, bokeh/orb decoration, and vague "boost productivity" copy.

## Layout

- Mobile-first: bottom navigation, thumb-friendly primary actions, large inputs, and short flows.
- Desktop: restrained app shell with sidebar and content grid.
- Forms should group each set as one compact row, with fast duplicate and remove actions.
- Empty states should say exactly what the user can do next.

## Components

- Buttons: solid graphite/green primary, quiet secondary, danger only for destructive actions.
- Inputs: high contrast, 8px radius, stable height, visible focus.
- Badges/chips: muscle groups, PRs, status, week labels.
- Charts: simple bars and rings only; no complex chart chrome.

## Voice

Spanish UI. Direct, calm, focused on training. No hype.
